ABOUT US

Our Mission and Vision

Our Mission: To achieve continuous success in all competitions while nurturing and developing future generations of athletes who embody the values of integrity, respect, and perseverance.

Our Vision: To become a globally recognized sports organization, renowned for our commitment to excellence, our vibrant fan culture, and our positive impact on the community.

Founders and Board of Directors

Since the club's founding in 2010 in Cairo, Egypt, a team of inspiring leaders has worked to build this athletic institution into a symbol of success and professionalism.

Founder: Ahmed El Sayed

Chairman of the Board: Mahmoud Ali

Vice Chairman: Omar Khaled

CEO: Sara Mohamed

Our Community Impact

At our club, we believe success extends beyond the field. We are committed to giving back to the community through our charitable programs, supporting local initiatives, and raising awareness for aspiring young athletes, to instill positive values in the next generation.

Monthly Activities

Charity Fun Run: Held on the first Saturday of every month to support a local education foundation

Family Movie Night: An outdoor movie screening on the last Friday of the month

Healthy Cooking Workshop: A monthly interactive workshop led by nutrition experts.

Open Ping Pong Tournament: A monthly competitive tournament for all ages
